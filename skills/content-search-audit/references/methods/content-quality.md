# Content quality and factual reliability

Adapted from Corey Haines' seo-audit content-quality checklist and Profound's
ai-answer-accuracy-auditor claim-verification workflow. See [NOTICE](../../NOTICE.md).

## Inputs and applicability

Apply to every readable content item. Require source snapshot and intended user
task; if the task is inferred, label it. For material factual claims, also require
dated supporting evidence. A page can be brief and still fully serve its task.

## Procedure

1. State the audience, task and intended next action in one sentence. Identify
   the passage supporting that interpretation rather than inventing a persona.
2. Inventory decision-relevant claims: price, availability, eligibility, results,
   limitations, compatibility, dates, comparisons and instructions. Preserve the
   exact locator and distinguish source assertions from auditor interpretation.
3. Compare each material claim to current authoritative evidence. Classify as
   supported-current, incomplete, ambiguous, stale, contradicted, subjective or
   unverifiable. A brand's preferred phrasing is not independent corroboration.
4. Evaluate whether the content enables the stated task: missing prerequisites,
   unclear steps, unexplained terminology, unsupported numbers, contradictions
   and inaccessible information can be actionable findings.
5. Identify actual added value: an example, first-hand result, methodology,
   explanation, useful tool or supported synthesis. Do not label material
   unoriginal solely because its wording resembles common industry language.
6. Inspect dates/version applicability, author/source transparency and evidence
   quality. Missing credentials justify an uncertainty, not a claim of fraud or
   lack of expertise. Do not use AI-writing detectors as authorship proof.
7. Review informative images, diagrams and transcripts for equivalent useful
   text. Decorative imagery may appropriately have empty alternatives.

## Output and verification

Record claim, source locator, verdict, evidence date, decision impact and proposed
correction. Separate factual errors from subjective criticism. Recheck the revised
claim against the same authoritative source and applicable product/version.
No automated rewrite or external correction outreach is authorized by this audit.

## Limitations

Absent source access makes a claim unverifiable, not false. Do not calculate a
quality score or forecast traffic from readability. Use a human/domain reviewer for
high-impact specialized claims. A sampled claim review must disclose its sample;
it cannot represent all claims as verified.
