# NKSkills product and campaign context

NKSkills builds versioned Solution Skills: customer-facing workflows composed of
original components and selected, pinned public Upstream Skills. Codex and Claude
Code are the only supported hosts.

The first audience is software engineers onboarding into an unfamiliar codebase.
Product owners are a future audience, not an established customer segment. The
current public CTA is to copy a starter prompt, with GitHub-based installation
instructions. No account, paid plan, direct-download archive, or analytics is
offered by the campaign site.

The technical release is `codebase-onboarding@1.1.0`. It has an independently
versioned manifest and recorded build/install checks. Behavior quality has not
been broadly validated on customer repositories. `problem-presentation@0.1.0`
is an evaluation candidate, not a commercial release. Do not invent customer
quotes, conversion metrics, usage counts, pricing, or guaranteed outcomes.
