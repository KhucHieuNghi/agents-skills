# Content Search Audit: customer-facing evidence

Status: approved by the owner with “Ok” after the concrete website scope was restated. Scope chosen by the owner: website summary with a detailed customer dossier.

## Intent

Help a commercial customer understand which upstream methods are adapted, which capabilities NKSkills owns, and exactly what the evaluation evidence does and does not establish.

## Approaches

A full public evidence page would expose every experimental detail in the buying flow. An internal-only dossier would leave the current provenance and quality questions unanswered. The owner selected a compact website summary plus a customer dossier. The repository is already public: customer-oriented does not mean confidential or access-controlled.

## Design

Add one reusable bilingual “Sources & verification” block to the Content Search Audit area of the landing and catalog. List the five upstream skill names, authors and pinned source links; state that these are selected method adaptations, with no upstream executable code. Explain the original NKSkills inventory/evidence/coverage/recovery/reporting layer. Give three bounded proof points: fresh installs/upgrades on both hosts; labeled semantic and clean-control trials with separate native/corrected outcomes; actual interruption and recovery of 1,200 declared items. Place the small-development-suite and mandatory-independent-review limitations alongside these facts. Link public attribution; keep the long benchmark tables and dossier out of the landing flow. No marketing accuracy score, general superiority claim, conversion uplift or customer guarantee.

The customer dossier will contain an executive brief, exact upstream-to-method mapping, original capability ownership, metric definitions and denominators, a scenario/result matrix, original-versus-assisted outcomes, evidence links and a proposed future comparative benchmark. Proposed tests and targets must never look like achieved results. Preserve historical failures. Explain that 525 root tests cover the repository rather than semantic audit quality.

Reuse existing typography, colors and responsive layout; no new dependency, tracking, form or unsupported host. Keep the 0.2.0 package and archive immutable. Record its stale candidate wording as a packaging issue requiring a separately versioned patch, without claiming it is fixed here.

## Verification

Source-check every displayed number and upstream link against the provenance lock and frozen receipts. Check both locales, mobile overflow, accessibility and truthful status wording; run required root checks plus site contract/type/browser checks. No new native model trials are claimed. Deployment is a separate observable result, never inferred from local edits.
