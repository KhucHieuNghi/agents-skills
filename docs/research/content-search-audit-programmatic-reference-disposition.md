# Corey programmatic SEO local-reference review

Read both local references and all seven eval cases at immutable revision
`1f837447b20bb6f0e72d87b80ece1c221c362c7d`; all inventoried hashes match the
[support-source receipt](content-search-audit-corey-support-review.json).
Cases were read, not executed. The source remains reference-only and excluded
from current inheritance.

The twelve playbooks provide a useful classification of content families: templates,
curation, conversion utilities, comparisons, examples, locations, personas,
integrations, glossaries, translations, directories and profiles. That taxonomy
can organize audit coverage; it does not establish demand, ranking, conversion
rate or a mandatory number of pages. Each page's actual task and evidence remain
the criteria. A conversion utility may need live currency data, whereas a fixed
unit conversion does not; a concise dictionary answer may satisfy its stated task.

The platform reference's unconditional initial-HTML requirement is too broad for
a universal search defect. Google documents JavaScript rendering and use of rendered
HTML, while recommending server/pre-rendering and noting that not every bot executes
JavaScript. Audit the actual surface and captured rendering evidence rather than
assuming all engines behave alike. [Google JavaScript SEO basics](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/javascript/javascript-seo-basics),
checked 2026-11-16, supports this distinction; it does not prove any customer's
page is rendered, indexed or selected.

Other exclusions:

- Platform item limits, export caps, refresh latency, pricing and deployment choices
  are dated vendor claims requiring separate current verification. No recommendation
  to purchase a platform or provision a runtime is inherited.
- The Ploy partner and authorship disclosure identifies additional lineage. Its
  integration guide, registry and Ploybook were not reviewed here. Do not treat the
  upstream partner designation as our endorsement.
- Fixed pilot sizes, highest-volume-first prioritization, all-page publication,
  automated noindex and mandatory linking/template patterns are not audit defaults.
- Higher conversion for personas, large local intent and lower competition in
  languages are hypotheses requiring market-specific observations, not forecasts.
- Evaluations 2 and 6 route to other skills; that conflicts with our single-entrypoint
  customer experience. Evaluation 4 gives thin content prominence before establishing
  the observed cause of non-indexing. Keep possible causes separate from findings.
- Evaluation 5's DefinedTerm suggestion does not prove eligibility for a Google
  search feature. Schema applicability and current feature rules require separate
  evidence. Evaluation 7 embeds changing platform assertions; it is not stable gold.

Useful comparison principles remain correct page-specific data, functional advertised
integrations, honest localization and explicit operational constraints. They already
fit the existing task/claims/portfolio contracts. No copied reference, new source pin,
platform dependency or runtime change is introduced by this review.
