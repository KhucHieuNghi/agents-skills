## Method-by-method assessment

| Upstream method                                           | Audit consequence                                                                                                                                                        |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Seven focused editing sweeps                              | Useful as review prompts, not seven universally required defects or seven required rewrite passes. Task-specific applicability comes first.                              |
| Preserve voice and justify each change                    | Compatible with audit proposals. Source text remains unchanged; absence of an improvement is a valid clean result.                                                       |
| Prove claims and avoid invented facts                     | Compatible. Nearby testimonials, logos or guarantees are not automatically evidence for every adjacent claim; preserve what each source actually supports.               |
| Specificity examples with numbers, deadlines and outcomes | Illustrative marketing examples are not customer facts. Do not fill a vague sentence with invented counts or performance gains. Missing proof stays missing.             |
| Benefit bridges, emotion and risk reversals               | Conditional on the reader's task and authorized policy. A status-code reference need not sell, evoke emotion or provide a refund guarantee.                              |
| Persona scores and publication thresholds                 | Exclude. Simulated panel scores do not supply calibrated quality measurement, independent review or authority to publish.                                                |
| Sentence, conjunction, fragment and punctuation quotas    | Exclude as deterministic quality rules. These are style preferences whose usefulness depends on audience and content.                                                    |
| AI-tell patterns                                          | May motivate an authorized style suggestion; cannot establish who wrote a passage or whether its claims are true. Preserve valid FAQ, factual lists and useful contrast. |
| Refresh by age or competitor frequency                    | Investigate with context. An old observation can remain correct within its historical scope; frequent competitor coverage does not prove reader demand.                  |
| Keep URL and anchors during refresh                       | Consider navigation and migration evidence. No automatic redirect, metadata update, schema insertion or publication follows from an audit proposal.                      |

These are scope and inference judgments, not claims that an upstream host always
fails in these ways. Several upstream safeguards explicitly prohibit inventing facts
and provide exceptions for legitimate style patterns; the narrower safeguards must
not disappear when comparing isolated examples or checklist rows.

## Local references and primary checks

`references/checklist.md` explicitly distinguishes broad editing from a point edit,
asks whether a change is better rather than merely different, and credits other
skills for its editing modes. It still includes marketing-specific emotion,
benefit and guarantee checks. Their absence is not an automatic defect across our
full content inventory. The cited Profound/Wispr derivation remains an external
lineage question; this pass does not clear their material for redistribution.

`references/content-refresh.md` suggests reviewing older statistics, following a
fixed cadence, and classifying subtopics by frequency among five competitors. Those
are candidate investigation triggers, not proof of staleness, causal ranking factors
or obligatory additions. An attributed 2024 study published in 2026 should retain
its collection period; replacing its dates with the current year changes the claim.
A current-price statement is a different case and needs the current policy source.

Google's date guidance distinguishes significant updates from merely changing a
date. Its News-specific section warns against artificial freshening; the broader
page guidance recommends consistent visible/structured dates for actual updates.
This supports accurate date reporting, not a universal age threshold or ranking
promise. [Google date guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2019/03/help-google-search-know-best-date-for).

`references/plain-english-alternatives.md` is an English suggestion list, not a
semantics-preserving replacement engine or Vietnamese language policy. For example,
changing “fabricated measurements” to “made measurements” can erase the distinction
between invented and observed data; replacing a software “render” operation with
“give” can lose its technical meaning. These are author-created linguistic controls,
not observed upstream executions. Audience and context determine whether a change
improves comprehension. The Australian Government Style Manual allows specialist
terms where audience research supports them and encourages explanations where
needed. [Official word-choice guidance](https://www.stylemanual.gov.au/writing-and-designing-content/clear-language-and-writing-style/plain-language-and-word-choice).

The table names multiple external sources. Only the current official word-choice
page was checked here; no row-by-row provenance or licensing reconciliation for the
