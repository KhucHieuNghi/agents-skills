## Selection rationale

The compare entrypoint conflicts with the measurement entrypoint's scope: the
latter asks for per-engine repeated observations, whereas compare accepts inferred
scores and forecasts business effects from score deltas. Their proximity in one
repository does not make those procedures compatible. Keep implementation change,
observed outcome and causal attribution separate. A missing score remains absent;
no overall score is part of our v1 contract.

The video method's exclusion of Shorts would directly violate full declared
inventory accounting. Retain every in-scope video, including unknown or unavailable
transcripts. Duration and automatic-caption origin do not alone establish a defect;
inspect the supplied language, meaning and task evidence. Video citations, channel
mentions and website referrals also require separate units and denominators.

Fanout-generated topics are candidate hypotheses. Label whether a question came
from customer evidence, analytics, an observed retrieval trace or model generation.
A missing page for an invented query is not automatically a material content gap.
Changing slugs is an implementation proposal requiring migration analysis, not a
read-only audit operation. Specific vendor-study percentages remain unverified
and excluded from inherited facts or forecasts.

The access workflow conflates controls and outcomes. Current IndexNow documentation
states that submission notifies engines and does not guarantee indexing. Its
presence therefore cannot prove ChatGPT inclusion, nor its absence prove exclusion.
[IndexNow FAQ](https://www.indexnow.org/faq).
Google also documents that `site:` results are not exhaustive, so a search result
count is not a complete index denominator. This Google-specific statement is not
being used as proof of Bing's exact implementation.
[Google site operator](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/monitor-debug/search-operators/all-search-site).
Crawler-role corrections remain supported by the primary references in the earlier
disposition; no blanket allow-training recommendation is inherited.
