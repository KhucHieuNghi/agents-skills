| Material                        | Useful comparison                                              | Boundary for the custom audit                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Centralized competitor records  | Track dated sources and shared facts across pages              | A shared record can spread one incorrect fact widely. Reuse does not establish accuracy; preserve field-level source scope, currency, billing cadence, geography and tier.                                                           |
| Illustrative competitor YAML    | Shows the fields a comparison might need                       | Product prices, limits, complaints, ratings and migration times are examples, not verified current facts. Do not seed an audit with them.                                                                                            |
| Feature table                   | Distinguishes absent listing from unavailable capability       | A trial only establishes behavior under its tested plan, permissions and configuration; it does not prove universal absence.                                                                                                         |
| Pricing and support templates   | Include plan limits, extras and scope of service               | A table's slots do not authorize inventing fees, SLA, support performance or annualized costs. A total-cost comparison needs the actual team size, included features and charge basis.                                               |
| Migration and social proof      | Preserve transfer limitations and attributed customer evidence | Do not manufacture switcher testimonials, migration commitments or measured results to fill a template.                                                                                                                              |
| Hub, footer and page generation | Compare intended navigation with observed links                | Fixed URL structures, footer-column counts, commercial CTAs and maximum eight links per column are not universal audit requirements. The document's search-equity and category-positioning claims are not customer outcome evidence. |
| Subjective ratings              | Potentially summarize a declared evaluation rubric             | Star ratings without criteria, observations and scope cannot become an objective comparison or our overall score.                                                                                                                    |

Case 7 correctly rejects absence claims inferred from a pricing page and unsupported
motives. Its expected output still asks to state a price change as a dated fact;
that needs evidence of the change, not merely the request's instruction to assert it.
Case 8's claim ledger and explicit changed/unverifiable/overclaimed states are useful,
but public-first ordering should not override evidenced severity in internal material.
Cases 1–5 largely reward content generation and commercial positioning. Case 4 adds
monetization advice; case 6 routes to another skill. These are outside the one-skill
read-only audit contract and are not imported as acceptance labels.

No live competitor facts, reviews, tools or ranking effects were independently
verified in this local-source review. Links to example products are not authorization
to fetch them or publish comparisons. The existing support-method fixture already
covers missing SSO listings versus documented availability and mixed billing scopes;
its development results do not certify every competitor-comparison scenario.
