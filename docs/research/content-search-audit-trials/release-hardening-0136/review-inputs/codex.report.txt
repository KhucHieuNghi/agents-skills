### F01 · High · SEO · Public product page sends noindex

> 01-visibility.md:3 — Synthetic fixture; audit only the supplied capture. Publisher intent: this public product page should be eligible for Google indexing.
> 01-visibility.md:5 — Robots.txt permits Googlebot access to /product. Response header: X-Robots-Tag: noindex.
> 01-visibility.md:6 — Raw HTML head: <meta name="robots" content="index,follow"><link rel="canonical" href="https://store.example.invalid/product">

The 2026-11-10 08:00 UTC capture for `/product` shows HTTP 200 and permitted crawl access. The header conflicts with the stated Google indexing intent. Crawl permission, the HTML directive and the self-canonical do not cancel the response-level noindex. Actual indexing and engine-selected canonical are unknown. The capture supplies no AI crawler or retrieval result.

**Action and verification.** Remove the conflicting response directive for `/product`. Verify the header, raw metadata and crawl policy in a fresh authorized capture. Verify actual Google indexing separately with a dated URL-level observation if supplied.

### F02 · High · SEO · Two routes redirect back to each other
