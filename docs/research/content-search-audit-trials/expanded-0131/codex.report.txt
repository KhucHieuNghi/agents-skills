### F01 · Response directive conflicts with Google indexing intent

**Affected item:** The public product page at `https://store.example.invalid/product` in dossier 01. **SEO and Google search readiness.**

> `01-visibility.md:3` — Synthetic fixture; audit only the supplied capture. Publisher intent: this public product page should be eligible for Google indexing.
> `01-visibility.md:4` — Capture 2026-11-10T08:00:00Z, https://store.example.invalid/product, HTTP 200.
> `01-visibility.md:5` — Robots.txt permits Googlebot access to /product. Response header: X-Robots-Tag: noindex.
> `01-visibility.md:6` — `Raw HTML head: <meta name="robots" content="index,follow"><link rel="canonical" href="https://store.example.invalid/product">`

In the 2026-11-10 capture, the HTTP response allows Googlebot to retrieve a 200 page but sends an explicit `noindex`. The HTML's permissive directive and self-canonical do not cancel the header. This conflicts with intended Google eligibility and creates a Google search readiness blocker. Actual index state, canonical selection and AI feature inclusion remain unknown.

**Action and verification.** Have web engineering trace and remove the unintended header for this product URL while retaining Googlebot access. Inspect fresh response headers, robots rules and raw and rendered directives for consistent policy. Use a subsequent supplied Google index observation to evaluate actual index state separately.

### F02 · Two product routes redirect to each other

**Affected items:** `/old` and `/new` in dossier 02. **SEO, retrieval readiness and journey.**

> `02-route.md:3` — Synthetic fixture. Publisher intent: /old should redirect once to an accessible /new product page.
> `02-route.md:5-6` — GET https://store.example.invalid/old -> 301 Location: https://store.example.invalid/new GET https://store.example.invalid/new -> 301 Location: https://store.example.invalid/old

The same-deployment pair forms a closed loop and prevents users and crawlers following this route from reaching product content. An older sitemap lists `/new` as preferred. It cannot establish the destination's availability or engine canonical selection. The registry's deterministic final-response check remains unknown because the trace has no terminal response.

**Action and verification.** Have web engineering serve `/new` directly and configure `/old` to redirect straight there. Inspect a fresh trace showing exactly one redirect from `/old` and direct HTTP 200 with the intended product content at `/new`. Compare the latest sitemap entry with the restored destination.

### F03 · Offer markup understates the confirmed price

**Affected item:** Example plan at `https://store.example.invalid/plan`, dossier 03. **SEO and AI search factual readiness.**

> `03-offer.md:3-4` — Synthetic fixture. Same page, market and capture time. The publisher confirms the visible US monthly price below is the current policy. Visible content: Example plan, United States monthly billing, USD 29 before taxes.
> `03-offer.md:12-17` —   "offers": {     "@type": "Offer",     "price": "19",     "priceCurrency": "USD",     "url": "https://store.example.invalid/plan"   }

The same-market, same-time JSON-LD Offer lists USD 19 while the publisher confirms USD 29 before taxes for US monthly billing. The visible price stands. Automated consumers of the markup can receive the wrong figure. The evidence establishes a discrepancy between representations. Feature eligibility, rich-result display and AI reuse remain unobserved.

**Action and verification.** Have the pricing and web owners update the matching Offer to USD 29, ideally from the same approved source as the visible price. Verify currency, monthly cadence, market, capture time and price basis in a fresh visible-copy and JSON-LD pair. Assess eligibility and actual search appearance separately if suitable evidence is supplied.

### F04 · Two current pages disagree on Starter storage

**Affected items:** `/product` and `/blog/starter-guide` in dossier 04. **Content quality, AIO, AEO and portfolio consistency.**

> `04-policy.md:3-6` — Synthetic fixture. Both pages describe the same Starter plan, market, monthly billing period and policy version 2026-10-01. /product: Starter includes 1000 storage units per month. /blog/starter-guide: Starter includes 1500 storage units per month. Both pages are labeled current. The authoritative entitlement table is unavailable. No query/page performance export is supplied.

Readers or downstream answers can receive incompatible entitlements for the same Starter plan, market, monthly billing period and policy version. The missing authoritative table prevents adjudicating either number. Overlap evidence supports correcting the contradiction. Query competition and duplication warranting consolidation remain unestablished.

**Action and verification.** Have product policy supply the approved entitlement row effective 2026-10-01 for the applicable market and period. Have content owners align both passages to it and retain the product and guide roles where each serves its task. Verify both revised passages against that same approved row and check that their units, period and applicability agree.

### F05 · Vietnamese ticket-change draft overstates the waiver and omits the deadline

**Affected item:** The Vietnamese draft in dossier 05. **Content quality and answer accuracy, with a downstream AIO readiness implication.**

> `05-huong-dan.md:4-5` — Bản nháp: Tất cả khách hàng được miễn phí đổi vé, không cần báo trước. Chính sách hiện hành: Chỉ hạng Thành viên được miễn phí đổi vé. Yêu cầu đổi phải gửi trước giờ khởi hành ít nhất 24 giờ. Các hạng khác áp dụng mức phí trong bảng giá riêng; bảng giá đó chưa được cung cấp.

The draft extends the waiver to all customers and says no notice is needed. The supplied policy limits the waiver to the Member tier and requires a request at least 24 hours before departure. Other tiers use a separate fee schedule whose prices are unavailable. The two errors affect the same ticket-change decision.

**Action and verification.** Have the Vietnamese content and policy owners align the answer to the supplied policy. Proposed Vietnamese copy:

> Chỉ hạng Thành viên được miễn phí đổi vé. Yêu cầu đổi phải được gửi trước giờ khởi hành ít nhất 24 giờ. Các hạng khác áp dụng mức phí theo bảng giá riêng.

Compare the revised copy with the policy for Member and other-tier customers, including requests exactly 24 hours before departure and requests made later. Use the separate approved schedule to verify any specific fee published later.
