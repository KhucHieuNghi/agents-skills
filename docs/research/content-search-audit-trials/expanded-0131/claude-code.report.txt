## Material findings

All five are observed in the supplied text. Each proposed action is a proposal for a separate task; nothing was changed.

### F1 — Response header `noindex` conflicts with the meta robots tag and the indexing intent (SEO, high)

Affected item: `targets/01-visibility.md` (capture of `https://store.example.invalid/product`).

- `01-visibility.md:3` — "Publisher intent: this public product page should be eligible for Google indexing."
- `01-visibility.md:5` — "Robots.txt permits Googlebot access to /product. Response header: X-Robots-Tag: noindex."
- `01-visibility.md:6` — `<meta name="robots" content="index,follow">`

**Rationale.** The header and the meta tag give opposite index directives, and the header contradicts the stated intent. Google's robots documentation, as I recall it, says the more restrictive directive applies when they conflict; I could not re-read it in this offline review. Because Googlebot is allowed to crawl the page, it can read the header.

**Not established.** Whether the page is indexed. `01-visibility.md:7` — "No Search Console or actual index observations are supplied."

**Proposed action.** Remove `noindex` from the `X-Robots-Tag` header for `/product`. If `noindex` is in fact intended, correct the stated intent and the meta tag instead.

**Verification.** Re-capture the response headers and raw HTML head for the URL and confirm that neither contains `noindex`. Index status then needs a separate index observation.

**Clean on the same item.** The declared canonical matches the captured URL (`01-visibility.md:6` — `<link rel="canonical" href="https://store.example.invalid/product">`), and the capture reports HTTP 200. Crawl access for Googlebot is consistent with the intent.

### F2 — `/old` and `/new` redirect to each other (SEO, high)

Affected item: `targets/02-route.md`.

- `02-route.md:3` — "Publisher intent: /old should redirect once to an accessible /new product page."
- `02-route.md:5` — "GET https://store.example.invalid/old -> 301 Location: https://store.example.invalid/new"
- `02-route.md:6` — "GET https://store.example.invalid/new -> 301 Location: https://store.example.invalid/old"

**Rationale.** The two captured responses form a loop, so the preferred URL `/new` never returns a final response in this capture. That contradicts the single-redirect intent and the sitemap preference for `/new`.

**Not established.** The engine-selected canonical, and whether the sitemap is still current. `02-route.md:7` — "An older sitemap export lists /new as the preferred URL. No final successful response or engine-selected canonical is available."

**Proposed action.** Remove the redirect from `/new` back to `/old` so that `/new` serves a final successful response, and keep the single 301 from `/old`.

**Verification.** Re-capture both URLs: `/old` returns one 301 to `/new`, and `/new` returns a final successful response with no `Location` pointing to `/old`. Check that the current sitemap lists `/new`.

**Helper limitation.** The loop is persisted under the canonical-targets rule. The deterministic HTTP-status rule stays unknown, because it cannot represent two non-final 301 responses.

### F3 — JSON-LD offer price contradicts the confirmed visible price (SEO + AIO, high)

Affected item: `targets/03-offer.md`. One issue, recorded once under two dimensions: structured data (SEO) and entity consistency (AIO).

- `03-offer.md:3` — "The publisher confirms the visible US monthly price below is the current policy."
- `03-offer.md:4` — "Visible content: Example plan, United States monthly billing, USD 29 before taxes."
- `03-offer.md:14` — `"price": "19",`
- `03-offer.md:15` — `"priceCurrency": "USD",`

**Rationale.** For the same page, market and capture time, the machine-readable price is 19 USD and the visible, publisher-confirmed price is USD 29. Anything that reads the markup gets a different price from a human reader.

**Not established.** Rich-result eligibility or appearance. `03-offer.md:21` — "No feature-eligibility test, Search Console or observed rich result is supplied."

**Proposed action.** Change `offers.price` from `19` to `29` for the US monthly offer and keep `priceCurrency` as `USD`. The dossier does not say whether structured-data prices should include tax; keep the visible "before taxes" basis unless the owner says otherwise.

**Verification.** Re-extract the JSON-LD from the revised page and compare `offers.price` and `priceCurrency` with the visible price. Feature eligibility is a separate check that was not run.

**Clean on the same item.** The visible price itself matches the publisher's confirmation.

### F4 — Two current pages state different Starter storage entitlements (Portfolio + AIO, with an open quality question, high)

Affected item: `targets/04-policy.md` (pages `/product` and `/blog/starter-guide`). One issue, recorded once: a cross-page contradiction (portfolio) that is also an entity inconsistency (AIO).

- `04-policy.md:3` — "Both pages describe the same Starter plan, market, monthly billing period and policy version 2026-10-01."
- `04-policy.md:4` — "/product: Starter includes 1000 storage units per month."
- `04-policy.md:5` — "/blog/starter-guide: Starter includes 1500 storage units per month."

**Rationale.** The scope is identical and both pages are labeled current, so the two values cannot both be right.

**Not established.** Which value is correct. `04-policy.md:6` — "Both pages are labeled current. The authoritative entitlement table is unavailable. No query/page performance export is supplied." The factual-accuracy check for each page therefore stays unknown, and so does any overlap or cannibalization between the two pages.

**Proposed action.** Investigate first: obtain the authoritative entitlement table for Starter, policy version 2026-10-01, then correct whichever page disagrees. Do not pick a value without that source.

**Verification.** Compare both revised statements with the entitlement table for the same plan, market, billing period and policy version. Both pages should state the same value.

### F5 — Bản nháp mâu thuẫn với chính sách đổi vé (Content quality + AEO, high)

Affected item: `targets/05-huong-dan.md` (Vietnamese draft). One issue, recorded once: an unsupported factual claim (quality) that is also a wrong answer to the customer's question (AEO).

- `05-huong-dan.md:4` — "Bản nháp: Tất cả khách hàng được miễn phí đổi vé, không cần báo trước."
- `05-huong-dan.md:5` — "Chính sách hiện hành: Chỉ hạng Thành viên được miễn phí đổi vé. Yêu cầu đổi phải gửi trước giờ khởi hành ít nhất 24 giờ. Các hạng khác áp dụng mức phí trong bảng giá riêng; bảng giá đó chưa được cung cấp."

**Rationale.** The draft is wrong on two points in one sentence. It extends free changes to all customers, where the policy limits them to the Thành viên tier. It also says no notice is needed, where the policy requires the request at least 24 hours before departure.

**Not established.** The fee amounts for other tiers, because the fee table was not supplied. Any traffic or conversion effect: `05-huong-dan.md:6` — "Không có dữ liệu traffic, thứ hạng hoặc tỷ lệ chuyển đổi."

**Proposed action** (proposed wording, not a quotation): "Chỉ hạng Thành viên được miễn phí đổi vé. Yêu cầu đổi phải gửi trước giờ khởi hành ít nhất 24 giờ. Các hạng khác áp dụng mức phí theo bảng giá riêng." Do not state fee amounts until the fee table is supplied.

**Verification.** Compare the revised sentence with the supplied policy for eligibility, the 24-hour condition and the fee-table reference. Check any fee amounts against the fee table once it is available.
