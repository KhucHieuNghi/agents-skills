### `denominator.md` — The draft uses the wrong denominator

**Evidence.** The study reports “Responses citing at least one top-ten result: 80,” “Total citation instances across responses: 400,” and “Citation instances pointing to top-ten results: 120.”

**Why it matters.** The 80% figure describes how often a *response* contained at least one qualifying citation. The share of *citation instances* pointing to top-ten results was 120/400, or 30%. The draft also generalizes from a fixed synthetic API prompt panel to the engine as a whole.

**Correction.** “In a fixed synthetic search API prompt panel collected 1–7 October 2026, 80 of 100 responses cited at least one top-ten result. Of 400 citation instances, 120 (30%) pointed to top-ten results.”

**Verification.** Recalculate both percentages from the study extract and check that each percentage names its unit, collection window, and surface.

### `data-age.md` — The publication date does not establish freshness

**Evidence.** The metadata lists “Publication date: 2026-11-01,” “Underlying collection: 2024-02-01 through 2024-02-14,” and “No later collection or current validation is supplied.”

**Why it matters.** The November 2026 report describes a February 2024 measurement. The 40% figure applies to the sampled English prompts on the synthetic API. The fixture provides no basis for a current rate.

**Correction.** “In a synthetic API sample collected 1–14 February 2024 using English prompts, 40 of 100 responses cited video sources.”

### `source-support.md` — The cited policy contradicts the price and storage claims

**Evidence.** The policy states “United States monthly plan: $29 before applicable taxes” and “Storage allowance: 100 GB. Other countries and annual pricing are not supplied.”

**Why it matters.** The policy gives $29 in place of $19 and caps storage at 100 GB. Its US scope leaves the worldwide pricing claim unsupported.

**Correction.** “Effective 1 October 2026, the Example plan costs $29 per month in the United States before applicable taxes and includes 100 GB of storage.”

**Verification.** Check the effective date, market, billing interval, tax qualifier, and storage allowance against the supplied policy. Broader pricing claims would require policy coverage for the markets and billing intervals named.

### `causal-drop.md` — The shared decline does not identify its cause

**Evidence.** The counts were “our domain 20 citations; competitor A 30; competitor B 50” before and “our domain 10 citations; competitor A 15; competitor B 25” after. The file says the “prompt file, collection success logs, engine configuration, publication changes and independent platform evidence were not retained.”

**Why it matters.** All three recorded counts fell 50%. That pattern could reflect a platform change, a change in prompt coverage, or collection failures. It also cannot rule out a site-specific problem.

**Correction.** “Recorded citations fell 50% week over week for our domain and both monitored competitors. The available records do not establish the cause or rule out site-specific issues.”

**Verification.** The supplied counts verify the decline. To investigate future changes, retain the prompt panel, engine configuration, collection success logs, and site publication history. Compare equivalent collections, check for site-specific changes, and seek independent evidence before attributing a decline to a platform change. The missing records prevent those checks for this interval.
