## Findings in dossier order

### F01 · High · Restore the required invitation step

**01, `/activate`** · Quality, AIO, AEO, portfolio. The draft begins “Sign in. Open Settings > Activation. Enter the one-time code.” The approved procedure includes “accepting the emailed invitation,” and the current card says “Accept your emailed invitation.” A newly invited editor following the draft can miss a required prerequisite. The card is supporting evidence in this same dossier. **Action and verification.** The documentation owner should insert invitation acceptance before sign-in and walk through the four approved steps in order with a newly invited editor test case. Preserve the concise card and the existing accurate steps. [Draft, approved procedure and card](targets/01-setup.md#L3-L14)
