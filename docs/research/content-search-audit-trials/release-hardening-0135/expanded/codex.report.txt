# Offline content and search audit

All ten synthetic dossiers were inspected as independent scenarios. The declared inventory also includes the two underlying media URLs in dossier I. **Coverage is partial: 12 declared and in scope, 0 fully audited, 10 pending, 2 blocked, 0 failed, 0 unsupported and 0 excluded.** The ten source files were collected. The two URL-only items were blocked by the offline scope. Discovery covers the declared inputs. It does not extend to linked resources, full deployments or sites behind the captures.

There are **five material findings**. Prioritize the redirect loop and indexing directive for the affected routes, and the two customer-facing offer and ticket-policy discrepancies before reuse or publication. Resolve the Starter conflict against authoritative product policy before choosing a value.

AIO below means AI search optimization and readiness. Google AI Overviews is a specific product. The audit assessed accessible signals, answer content and task relationships where supplied. It observed no current engine behavior. The historical study attached to this audit reports **40 of 100 responses** citing video sources in a synthetic English API panel collected February 1–14, 2024 and published November 1, 2026.

## Material findings and verification

### F1. Noindex conflicts with declared indexing intent · High · SEO

Affected item: A, `https://store.example.invalid/product`, captured 2026-11-10 at 08:00 UTC.

- 01-visibility.md:3 — `Publisher intent: this public product page should be eligible for Google indexing.`
- 01-visibility.md:5 — `Robots.txt permits Googlebot access to /product. Response header: X-Robots-Tag: noindex.`
- 01-visibility.md:6 — `Raw HTML head: <meta name="robots" content="index,follow"><link rel="canonical" href="https://store.example.invalid/product">`

The header supplies a Google indexing exclusion despite HTTP 200, the permissive robots rule, the HTML directive and the self-canonical. This establishes a captured eligibility conflict, with implications for Google AI surfaces that depend on eligible indexed pages. Actual crawling, indexing, canonical selection and AI inclusion remain unknown.

**Action and verification.** Have the web platform owner trace and remove the conflicting header from the intended public route. In an authorized fresh capture, check the effective header, HTML directives and intended Googlebot access on that exact URL. Verify actual Google index status independently with dated publisher-supplied evidence.

### F2. Both redirect endpoints send users back to each other · High · SEO and access readiness

Affected item: B, `https://store.example.invalid/old` and `https://store.example.invalid/new`.

- 02-route.md:3 — `Publisher intent: /old should redirect once to an accessible /new product page.`
- 02-route.md:5 — `GET https://store.example.invalid/old -> 301 Location: https://store.example.invalid/new`
- 02-route.md:6 — `GET https://store.example.invalid/new -> 301 Location: https://store.example.invalid/old`
- 02-route.md:7 — `An older sitemap export lists /new as the preferred URL. No final successful response or engine-selected canonical is available.`

The same-deployment responses establish a two-hop cycle rather than a terminating journey to the replacement page. The older sitemap documents a preference. Final destination content and engine canonical selection remain unknown.

**Action and verification.** Have the routing owner serve the product successfully at `/new` and keep one permanent hop from `/old` to that endpoint. Verify with a direct request to each route on the same deployment. Record each response status and Location and confirm that `/new` ends in HTTP 200 with the intended product content. Check canonical and sitemap alignment separately against current captures.

### F3. Offer markup understates the approved price · High · SEO and AIO

Affected item: C, the Example plan Offer for `https://store.example.invalid/plan`.

- 03-offer.md:3 — `The publisher confirms the visible US monthly price below is the current policy.`
- 03-offer.md:4 — `Visible content: Example plan, United States monthly billing, USD 29 before taxes.`
- 03-offer.md:14-15 — `"price": "19", "priceCurrency": "USD",`

The machine-readable Offer and the visible, approved US monthly offer disagree on the price in the same capture. The visible price has explicit publisher support. Feature eligibility and displayed rich results remain unknown.

**Action and verification.** Have the commerce owner align the structured Offer to USD 29 for the same market, monthly billing period and pre-tax basis, ideally using the same price source. Compare the visible and parsed structured values in a fresh same-market capture against the approved policy. If feature eligibility or appearance matters, collect separate evidence for each.

### F4. Two current Starter pages disagree on storage entitlement · High · Quality, AIO, AEO and portfolio

Affected item: D, both `/product` and `/blog/starter-guide` in this scenario.

- 04-policy.md:3 — `Both pages describe the same Starter plan, market, monthly billing period and policy version 2026-10-01.`
- 04-policy.md:4 — `/product: Starter includes 1000 storage units per month.`
- 04-policy.md:5 — `/blog/starter-guide: Starter includes 1500 storage units per month.`
- 04-policy.md:6 — `Both pages are labeled current. The authoritative entitlement table is unavailable. No query/page performance export is supplied.`

This is one contradiction affecting the quota buyers or customers can reasonably expect and the answer a system could extract. The authoritative quantity is unknown. The product page and guide may serve distinct tasks. The evidence leaves duplication worth consolidating and query cannibalization unresolved.

**Action and verification.** Have the product policy owner obtain the entitlement table for the stated version and scope. Once obtained, align both passages to that record and verify the two revised values, units, billing period, market and effective version against it. Keep both page purposes under review while investigating the shared claim.

### F5. Vietnamese draft overstates eligibility for free ticket changes · High · Quality, AIO and AEO

Affected item: E, the Vietnamese draft ticket-change answer.

- 05-huong-dan.md:4 — `Bản nháp: Tất cả khách hàng được miễn phí đổi vé, không cần báo trước.`
- 05-huong-dan.md:5 — `Chính sách hiện hành: Chỉ hạng Thành viên được miễn phí đổi vé. Yêu cầu đổi phải gửi trước giờ khởi hành ít nhất 24 giờ. Các hạng khác áp dụng mức phí trong bảng giá riêng; bảng giá đó chưa được cung cấp.`

The draft extends the benefit to all customers and removes the notice requirement. Both changes materially misstate the supplied current policy for Vietnamese readers deciding whether and when to request a change.

**Action and verification.** Proposed Vietnamese copy:

> Chỉ hạng Thành viên được miễn phí đổi vé. Vui lòng gửi yêu cầu đổi vé trước giờ khởi hành ít nhất 24 giờ. Các hạng khác áp dụng mức phí theo bảng giá riêng.

Have the Vietnamese content owner compare the revised answer against the supplied policy for Thành viên versus other tiers and for requests at exactly 24 hours versus less than 24 hours. Verify any fee figures against the separate schedule once supplied. Fee amounts remain unknown.
