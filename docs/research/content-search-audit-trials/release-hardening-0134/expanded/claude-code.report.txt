- **Targets and skill unchanged.** The ten target hashes after the audit equal the hashes collected at the start. No other skill was invoked and no tool was installed.
- **Platform guidance.** Statements about search-engine behaviour rely on the documentation links packaged in the rule registry (registry review date 2026-11-16). They were not re-read during this offline audit.

### F1 — Product page intended for Google indexing is served with a `noindex` header

Dossier 01 · SEO · severity high · confidence high · observed

- **01-visibility.md:3** — `Publisher intent: this public product page should be eligible for Google indexing.`
- **01-visibility.md:5** — `Robots.txt permits Googlebot access to /product. Response header: X-Robots-Tag: noindex.`
- **01-visibility.md:6** — `Raw HTML head: <meta name="robots" content="index,follow"><link rel="canonical" href="https://store.example.invalid/product">`
- **01-visibility.md:7** — `No Search Console or actual index observations are supplied.`

**Rationale.** The response header tells search engines not to index the page, while the meta tag says the opposite and the publisher wants the page indexable. Google's documented handling is that `noindex` can be delivered by header or meta tag and that the more restrictive directive applies when they conflict (recalled from the registry's linked guidance; not re-read offline). The captured response therefore contradicts the stated intent.

**What is not established.** Whether the page is or is not in Google's index. Crawl permission is not proof of a fetch or of indexing.

**Clean components.** The capture reports HTTP 200, the canonical is self-referential with no competing declaration, and robots.txt permits Googlebot.

**Proposed action.** Stop sending `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` on `https://store.example.invalid/product`, after finding where the header is set (server, CDN or application).

**Verification.** Capture the response headers and raw HTML head for the same URL again and confirm that no `noindex` value appears in either place. Indexing itself needs URL-level index evidence from the property owner; a Search Console workflow for that was not verified in this offline audit.

### F2 — `/old` and `/new` redirect to each other

Dossier 02 · SEO · severity high · confidence high · observed

- **02-route.md:3** — `Publisher intent: /old should redirect once to an accessible /new product page.`
- **02-route.md:5** — `GET https://store.example.invalid/old -> 301 Location: https://store.example.invalid/new`
- **02-route.md:6** — `GET https://store.example.invalid/new -> 301 Location: https://store.example.invalid/old`
- **02-route.md:7** — `An older sitemap export lists /new as the preferred URL. No final successful response or engine-selected canonical is available.`

**Rationale.** The two captured responses, from the same deployment and time, form a loop. The intended destination `/new` never returns a page, so neither a visitor nor a crawler can reach the product content, and the redirect on `/new` contradicts both the stated intent and the sitemap's preferred URL.

**What is not established.** The engine-selected canonical, and whether the sitemap export is still current (it is described only as older, with no date). The helper's transport check (`seo-response`) stays unknown because no final response exists to evaluate; the loop is recorded under the canonical-conflict check.

**Proposed action.** Remove the 301 from `/new` to `/old` so `/new` serves the product page, keeping the single 301 from `/old` to `/new`.

**Verification.** Request both URLs again without following redirects: `/old` should return one 301 to `/new`, and `/new` should return a successful final response with no `Location` header. Then confirm the current sitemap still lists `/new`.

### F3 — Structured-data price contradicts the confirmed visible price (consolidated: SEO, AIO, quality)

Dossier 03 · SEO + AIO + content quality · severity high · confidence high · observed

- **03-offer.md:3** — `The publisher confirms the visible US monthly price below is the current policy.`
- **03-offer.md:4** — `Visible content: Example plan, United States monthly billing, USD 29 before taxes.`
- **03-offer.md:14** — `"price": "19",`
- **03-offer.md:15** — `"priceCurrency": "USD",`
- **03-offer.md:21** — `No feature-eligibility test, Search Console or observed rich result is supplied.`

**Rationale.** For the same page, market and capture time, people see USD 29 while the JSON-LD `Offer` declares 19. This is one defect with three consequences, reported once:

- SEO: the structured data does not match the visible offer.
- AIO: the page's machine-readable and human-readable price facts for the same product disagree.
- Quality: the page carries a price claim that the publisher's own confirmed policy contradicts.

**Clean component.** The visible price is supported by the publisher's confirmation.

**What is not established.** Rich-result eligibility, any displayed rich result, and any actual engine or AI answer quoting the wrong price. Whether the structured price should also express the monthly period or the tax basis was not tested.

**Proposed action.** Proposed: change `offers.price` from `"19"` to `"29"`, and find the source of the 19 value so the two cannot drift again.

**Verification.** Re-extract the JSON-LD from a new capture of the same page and market, and compare `offers.price` and `priceCurrency` with the visible price and the confirmed policy.

### F4 — Two pages labeled current state different Starter allowances (consolidated: portfolio, AIO)

Dossier 04 · portfolio + AIO · severity medium · confidence high · observed · action: investigate

- **04-policy.md:3** — `Both pages describe the same Starter plan, market, monthly billing period and policy version 2026-10-01.`
- **04-policy.md:4** — `/product: Starter includes 1000 storage units per month.`
- **04-policy.md:5** — `/blog/starter-guide: Starter includes 1500 storage units per month.`
- **04-policy.md:6** — `Both pages are labeled current. The authoritative entitlement table is unavailable. No query/page performance export is supplied.`

**Rationale.** Plan, market, billing period and policy version are the same, so the usual legitimate reasons for different numbers are ruled out by the dossier. Both values cannot be right, and a customer or an AI system reading either page may get the wrong entitlement.

**What is not established.** Which page is wrong. Severity is medium rather than high only because the direction and size of the error cannot be fixed until the table is available. The quality claim check stays unknown for the same reason. Duplication or cannibalization between the two pages is not assessed: there is no query/page export.

**Proposed action.** Obtain the authoritative entitlement table for policy version 2026-10-01, then correct whichever page (or both) differs from it. No page is nominated for change yet.

**Verification.** Compare the allowance sentence on both revised pages with that table for the same plan, market, billing period and policy version.

### F5 — Draft promises free ticket changes to everyone with no notice; the policy does not

Dossier 05 (Vietnamese) · content quality · severity high · confidence high · observed

- **05-huong-dan.md:4** — `Bản nháp: Tất cả khách hàng được miễn phí đổi vé, không cần báo trước.`
- **05-huong-dan.md:5** — `Chính sách hiện hành: Chỉ hạng Thành viên được miễn phí đổi vé. Yêu cầu đổi phải gửi trước giờ khởi hành ít nhất 24 giờ. Các hạng khác áp dụng mức phí trong bảng giá riêng; bảng giá đó chưa được cung cấp.`
- **05-huong-dan.md:6** — `Không có dữ liệu traffic, thứ hạng hoặc tỷ lệ chuyển đổi.`

**Rationale.** The draft contradicts the supplied current policy on three points: who qualifies (all customers versus the Thành viên tier only), notice (none versus at least 24 hours before departure), and cost for other tiers (free versus a fee from a separate table). Published as written, it would promise customers something the policy does not grant.

**What is not established.** The fee amounts, because the fee table is not supplied. AEO was not assessed: the task is limited to comparing the draft with the policy and no customer question set is supplied.

**Proposed action.** Proposed wording, in the dossier's language and limited to what the policy states: "Chỉ hạng Thành viên được miễn phí đổi vé. Yêu cầu đổi phải gửi trước giờ khởi hành ít nhất 24 giờ. Các hạng khác áp dụng mức phí theo bảng giá riêng." Do not state fee amounts until the table is supplied.

**Verification.** Compare the revised sentence with the policy clause by clause (eligible tier, 24-hour notice, fees for other tiers), and check any fee amount against the fee table once supplied.
