## Constructing each report finding

Build each finding in this order:

1. **Observed:** name the affected artifact, the exact supported defect and its
   evidence. Include the capture surface and scope that make the comparison valid.
2. **Interpretation:** state what the observation establishes. Give downstream
   consequences as conditional risks. Put an untraced cause in an explicitly
   labelled hypothesis, with the evidence needed to distinguish it from alternatives.
3. **Correction:** supply the smallest source-supported replacement or repair.
   Carry over the authoritative record's material qualifiers. A proposed assertion
   about a platform prerequisite includes the specific feature and verified basis;
   when that basis is unavailable, keep eligibility unverified and give the direct
   evidence check instead.
4. **Verification:** describe the observable result that would demonstrate the
   correction, separately from any later engine outcome.

Then derive the executive summary from the finished findings and coverage records.
Use their actual IDs/counts and separate confirmed defects, clean checks and unknown
checks. Name the artifact being corrected: a valid page/markup can coexist with
an unsupported draft claim about it. Each summary recommendation has the same
scope and status as its detailed finding.
