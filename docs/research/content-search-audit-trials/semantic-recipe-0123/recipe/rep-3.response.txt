# Content, SEO, AEO and GEO audit (offline dossier review)

## Executive summary

This is a partial audit of the supplied dossiers only. I used no tools, ran no quote verifier, and reopened no files. Quotes are copied from the dossier text and have not been checked against source snapshots. I did no calculations, so no derived figures appear.

I found 5 material issues:
- The `/monthly` title states the wrong price.
- The rendered `/instructions` page omits a required step.
- The `/offer-a` JSON-LD is syntactically invalid.
- The draft observation conclusion overreaches.
- The draft claim about `/offer-b` overreaches.

Two checks are clean:
- `/export` has no demonstrated defect.
- The A→B descriptive increase is supported.

`/dashboard` is unknown.
