# Content, SEO, AEO and GEO Audit (offline, partial)

## Executive summary
This audit covers six supplied pages and one observation set. It is **partial**: the site inventory was not declared, `/dashboard` is unresolved, and no engine, index or live-validator evidence exists. Findings are based only on the supplied dossiers.

- **Confirmed defects (5):** F1 `/monthly` title price, F2 `/instructions` rendered step omitted, F3 unsupported causal claim in the draft conclusion, F4 `/offer-a` invalid JSON-LD, F5 `/offer-b` draft guarantee.
- **Clean:** `/export` title, the A-to-B descriptive comparison, and `/offer-b` markup on the supplied checks.
- **Unknown:** `/dashboard` rendering and indexability, and feature eligibility for both offers.

## Checks without findings
- **`/export` title:** it matches the page's task and the owner's intent. No screenshots, query data, character-limit requirement or truncation evidence was supplied, so no defect is claimed. Shortening it would be an unevidenced preference.
- **A-to-B change:** a rise from 2/10 to 4/10 is supported as a descriptive difference within that cohort. Only its persistence, generalization and cause are unknown.
- **`/dashboard`:** the raw HTML has only an empty root div. With no scripts, rendered snapshot, owner task or readiness signal, content quality and rendering stay **unknown**. The next step is an authorized rendered capture with an owner-specified ready signal.

## Verification limits
I used no tools, files, system clock, quote verifier or live validators. Quotations come only from the supplied dossier text and were not checked against source files or line ranges. The one calculation, the 2/10 to 4/10 comparison, is simple counts and was done by hand. No feature or tool availability was verified, so none is prescribed. Observation counts are synthetic, so no real visibility is measured.
