**Verification limits:** Supplied dossiers only. No browsing, tools, helpers, validators or quote-verifier receipt were available. Quotes below were checked by re-reading the dossiers, not by the helper. No counts are computed beyond supplied ones. Coverage is partial: six declared pages (/monthly, /export, /instructions, /dashboard, /offer-a, /offer-b) plus one observation set.

## Executive summary

Four material findings: a wrong price in the /monthly title, a raw-vs-rendered instruction mismatch on /instructions, an invalid JSON-LD block on /offer-a, and two overreaching draft claims (/offer-b guarantee; observation comparison). Clean: /export title, the A→B descriptive increase. Unknown: /dashboard rendering and indexing, and all engine eligibility or appearance.
