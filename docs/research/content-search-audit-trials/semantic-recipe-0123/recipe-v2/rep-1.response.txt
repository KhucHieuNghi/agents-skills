# Content, SEO, AEO and GEO Audit (offline, supplied dossiers only)

**Verification limits:** No tools, fetching, validators, rendering or engine checks were used. Quotes are from the supplied text only. No numeric aggregates are claimed. Coverage is limited to the six supplied pages (/monthly, /export, /instructions, /dashboard, /offer-a, /offer-b) and observation sets A, B and D.

## Executive summary

Four findings need action: a wrong price in the /monthly title (F1), an instruction that omits a required step in the rendered /instructions page (F2), malformed JSON-LD on /offer-a (F3), and two overreaching claims in the draft observation conclusion and the /offer-b draft (F4, F5). /export, the A→B comparison and the /offer-b markup are clean. /dashboard is unknown.

## Findings

| ID | Evidence and explanation | Correction | Verification |

The cause is untraced; a script that alters the text is a hypothesis, to be tested by comparing the template with the post-render DOM. | "To disable renewal, open Billing and select Disable renewal." | Re-capture the rendered DOM after the ready signal and confirm the full sentence. |

Eligibility and engine behavior are unverified. | `{"@context":"https://schema.org","@type":"Product","name":"Example"}` | Parse the script with a JSON parser in both raw and rendered output.
