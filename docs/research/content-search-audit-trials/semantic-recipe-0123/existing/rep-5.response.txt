# Content, SEO, AEO and GEO Audit (offline, bounded)

**Scope and limits.** This covers six supplied pages (/monthly, /export, /instructions, /dashboard, /offer-a, /offer-b) and one observation comparison. It is not a full-site audit, so no full-coverage claim applies. I ran no tools, quote verifier, validator or parser. Quotations were checked by manual re-reading of the supplied text only. No engine rendering, indexing or feature-eligibility evidence exists. All observation counts are synthetic and reviewer-supplied.

## Executive summary

Three pages have confirmed defects. /monthly has a title that contradicts its content. /instructions loses a required step in the rendered DOM. /offer-a has a trailing comma in its JSON-LD. The A-to-B observation increase is supported as a descriptive result. The draft's A-versus-D causal claim is not. /export is clean, /dashboard is unresolved, and the /offer-b draft claim overstates its evidence.
