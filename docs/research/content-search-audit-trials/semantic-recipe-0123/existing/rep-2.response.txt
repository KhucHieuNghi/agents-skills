# Content, SEO, AEO and GEO audit (offline, supplied dossiers only)

## Executive summary
Four material issues: a wrong price in the `/monthly` title, a missing step in the rendered `/instructions` content, invalid JSON-LD on `/offer-a`, and two overreaching draft claims (observation comparison, `/offer-b`). `/export` is clean. `/dashboard` is unresolved because no rendered evidence exists. The A→B rise (2/10 to 4/10) is a supported descriptive result and stays clean.

**Coverage: partial.** Six pages are in scope. Five were assessed on supplied evidence, and `/dashboard` is pending. This is not a full-site audit, and no discovery was performed.
