## Findings

### F1 · Fir SSO availability is misstated
**High priority, high confidence · 01 /compare assertion A · Content quality, AIO, AEO**

> Fir has no SSO, as its pricing table proves. — 01-competitor-claims.md:17
> SAML SSO is available on Enterprise, with a minimum of 20 paid seats. — 01-competitor-claims.md:13

The pricing excerpt omits SSO, and the supplied SSO documentation explicitly establishes Enterprise availability in the November 2026 US scenario. Availability for other plans is unknown. The blanket claim could cause buyers to rule out a viable option. The comparison editor should replace it with the documented Enterprise statement and cite the supplied SSO documentation. Verify that the revision preserves SAML, Enterprise and the minimum of 20 paid seats and makes no inference about other plans.

### F2 · Annual pricing reverses the draft comparison
**High priority, high confidence · 01 /compare assertion B · Content quality, AIO, AEO, portfolio**

> Cedar is cheaper per seat than Fir on every billing option. — 01-competitor-claims.md:18
> Our Cedar plan: USD 12 per seat per month, billed monthly, no seat minimum. — 01-competitor-claims.md:6
> Our annual option: USD 10 per seat per month equivalent, billed annually. — 01-competitor-claims.md:7
> Competitor Fir monthly option: USD 15 per seat per month, billed monthly. — 01-competitor-claims.md:8
> Fir annual option: USD 9 per seat per month equivalent, billed annually. — 01-competitor-claims.md:9

For the supplied November 2026 US plans, Cedar is USD 3 less per seat per month on monthly billing, and Fir is USD 1 less per seat per month equivalent on annual billing. Figures are before tax, with optional services excluded and none required for this comparison (01-competitor-claims.md:3-10). The comparison editor should present separate billing rows and preserve the per-seat basis. Verify both subtractions and the monthly caption against those rows. Keep total cost and feature equivalence outside the claim.

### F3 · Monthly cost per conversion mixes observation windows
**High priority, high confidence · 02 Draft A · Content quality, AEO, AIO**

> October cost per conversion was USD 85.71, from 3000 divided by 35. — 02-measurement-windows.md:10
> Spend is complete for October 1–31: USD 3000. — 02-measurement-windows.md:5
> Conversion export covers only October 1–7: 35 completed conversions. — 02-measurement-windows.md:6

The division rounds as stated, but the numerator covers all of October 2026 while the denominator covers seven days. Oct 8–31 conversions are missing. For this one campaign the reporting analyst should use the existing verified-window result of USD 20 per completed conversion for Oct 1–7, with the rest of the month unmeasured. Verify the same campaign, currency, period and conversion definition for USD 700 and 35 completed conversions. Publish a full-month figure only once aligned full-month conversion data are available.

### F4 · Identified referrals are extrapolated without evidence
**Medium priority, high confidence · 02 Draft B and independent referral report · Content quality, AEO, AIO, GEO measurement interpretation**

> These 15 tracked sessions prove AI assistants generated 150 sessions in total, because only one in ten AI referrals can be tracked. — 02-measurement-windows.md:17-18
> Independent referral report: 15 October sessions have an identified AI-assistant referrer. — 02-measurement-windows.md:14-15

Other session origins are unknown. The dossier supplies no tracking-rate model, complete session count, incrementality test or conversion linkage (02-measurement-windows.md:14-21). The analyst should report 15 identified October sessions as a synthetic observed count, remove the multiplier and causal wording, and keep this report independent of the campaign conversion counts. Verify the wording against the supplied count, period and referrer classification. A total estimate would need validated tracking coverage and uncertainty. A causal claim would need suitable incrementality evidence.

### F5 · Current South delivery passage gives the wrong service
**High priority, high confidence · 03 /south-delivery · Content quality, AIO, AEO, portfolio**

Verify the complete revised South passage against approved policy, North against its own cutoff and region, and the 2024 archive against the historic task and the owner-reported links for new orders.
