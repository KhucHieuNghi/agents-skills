# Content, SEO, AEO and GEO Audit

## Executive summary

Four issues are supported by the evidence. `/monthly` has a title price that contradicts both its body and the approved price. `/instructions` loses a required step in the rendered DOM. `/offer-a` has invalid JSON-LD. The draft observation conclusion overstates what the data shows. Several other items cannot be judged from the supplied evidence and are listed as not-a-finding.

## Not findings or unsupported claims

- **`/export` title:** No truncation, snippet or performance data was supplied. The title matches the page's content, and the owner wants the full task represented. Keep it unless evidence of truncation or poor performance appears. Do not assume a character limit.
- **`/dashboard`:** The raw HTML has only an empty root div. There is no rendered snapshot, owner task or readiness signal, and no engine rendering evidence. Whether the content is missing for users or engines cannot be determined. Obtain an authorized rendered capture and the owner's ready signal.
- **`/offer-b`:** JSON-LD syntax and the price match are confirmed. The draft claim that this "guarantees a rich result" is unsupported. Eligibility depends on feature-specific requirements and the engine's decision. Say only that the markup is syntactically valid and consistent with the visible content.
