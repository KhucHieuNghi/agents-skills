# Content, SEO, AEO and GEO Audit (offline, evidence-limited)

## Executive summary

Four findings are material. `/monthly` has a title that contradicts the approved price. `/instructions` loses a required step in the rendered DOM. The GEO comparison supports only a narrow claim, and the draft causal conclusion should be withdrawn. `/offer-a` has malformed JSON-LD. Other items (the `/export` title, `/dashboard`, `/offer-b`) need no change or only more evidence. No pages were fetched or altered.

## Items not raised as findings

- **`/export`:** The title fully matches the page task, and the owner wants it kept. No truncation or performance data exists, so shortening would be speculation.
- **`/dashboard`:** The raw HTML is an empty root div, but there is no rendered snapshot, readiness signal or task. Content cannot be judged. Supply an authorized rendered capture before assessing it.
- **`/offer-b`:** JSON-LD is valid and matches visible content. The claim "valid syntax and matching price guarantee a rich result" should be reworded: "The markup is syntactically valid and consistent with visible content. Rich-result eligibility and appearance are unverified."

## Limits

All GEO counts are synthetic reviewer-supplied figures. No engine-rendering, indexing, citation or conversion evidence was supplied.
