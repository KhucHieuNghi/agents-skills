# Content, SEO, AEO and GEO Audit (offline, evidence-only)

## Executive summary
Two pages carry material, verifiable defects: /monthly has a title that contradicts its body and the approved price, and /instructions shows a rendered DOM that omits a required step. /offer-a has a JSON-LD syntax error. The B-over-A visibility change is a descriptive observation only; the draft causal and market-wide claims are unsupported. /export, /dashboard and /offer-b need no change on current evidence.

## Material findings

| # | Page | Evidence | Why it matters | Replacement / remediation | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | /monthly | Title says USD 19; main content and approved price say USD 29 before tax. | Wrong price in snippets, answer engines and citations; trust and compliance risk. | Title: "Basic plan: USD 29 per month before tax" | Re-check title against approved price; confirm title and body agree after publish. |

## Reviewed, no finding
- **/export:** The title matches the full task and the owner wants all of it kept. No truncation or performance data exists, so shortening would be speculative.
- **/dashboard:** The raw HTML has only an empty root div, with no rendered snapshot, task or ready signal. A content defect cannot be concluded. Capture a rendered state with an owner-defined ready signal before judging.

## Limits
There was no browsing, no live validation and no engine rendering or index data. All observation counts are synthetic and reviewer-supplied.
