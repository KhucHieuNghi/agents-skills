# Content, SEO, AEO and GEO Audit

## Executive summary

The evidence supports four actionable items and several claims that should be withdrawn or softened. The `/monthly` title contradicts the visible and approved price. `/instructions` loses a required step in the rendered DOM. `/offer-a` has invalid JSON-LD. The draft visibility conclusion overreaches. `/export` and `/offer-b` need no change on the evidence supplied. `/dashboard` cannot be judged.

## Pages with no finding

- **/export:** The title matches the page content and includes all three fields. No truncation, character-limit or performance evidence exists, so there is no basis to shorten it. Keep the title and monitor it if that data becomes available.
- **/dashboard:** The raw HTML has only an empty root div. There is no rendered snapshot, owner task or ready signal, and no engine rendering or index evidence. A content-visibility defect is possible but unproven. Request a rendered capture with a ready signal before judging.

## Limits

All observation counts are synthetic, reviewer-supplied and not live measurements. No fetching or validators were used.
