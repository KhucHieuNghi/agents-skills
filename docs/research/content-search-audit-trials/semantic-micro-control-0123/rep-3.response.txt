# Content, SEO, AEO and GEO Audit

## Executive summary

The supplied evidence supports four material findings and rules out several overreaching claims. `/monthly` has a title that contradicts its approved price. `/instructions` shows a rendered step that omits a required action. `/offer-a` has structured data that is malformed in the supplied bytes. The GEO draft conclusion overstates what the synthetic observations can show. `/export`, `/dashboard` and `/offer-b` need no change on the evidence provided, beyond the caveats noted.

## Items needing no change

- **`/export`:** The title matches the page content. No truncation or performance data was supplied and no character limit is required, so I would not shorten it.
- **`/dashboard`:** The raw HTML is an empty root div, but no rendered snapshot, scripts, task or readiness signal exists. I cannot judge its content or indexability. Supplying a rendered capture is the next step.
- **`/offer-b`:** The JSON is valid and matches the visible name and price. The claim that valid syntax and matching price "guarantee a rich result" is unsupported. Eligibility depends on feature-specific requirements and engine decisions. Suggested wording: "The markup is syntactically valid and consistent with visible content. Eligibility is untested."

## Limits

This audit uses only the supplied dossiers. No fetching, validators or engine checks were run, and all observation counts are synthetic.
